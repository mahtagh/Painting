About

I'm a painter based in Calgary. I started taking painting classes to make time for something slow and hands-on, and I haven't stopped since.

I paint in acrylic, often with a palette knife and thick texture you can see and feel: foam on a wave, petals in someone's hair. My favourite subjects are the sea, flowers, and people caught in a quiet moment.

Commissions

I take a small number of commissions each year: a beach or landscape that matters to you, or a painting in colours to suit your room. Tell me what you have in mind and the size you'd like, and I'll reply with a price and timeline.
